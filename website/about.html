I’m Owen Henry, a student at the University of Wisconsin– Madison majoring in Economics and Data Science, with a certificate in Computer Science. My passion lies at the intersection of finance, analytics, and technology. I’ve built financial models to forecast business outcomes, analyzed economic data from the Federal Reserve, and worked on web development projects for both nonprofits and manufacturers. Through internships and certifications with institutions like General Mills, J.P. Morgan, and the Institute for Research on Poverty, I’ve honed my skills in data visualization, investment analysis, and strategic forecasting. I’m excited to apply these tools to solve real-world problems in the food and manufacturing sectors.
